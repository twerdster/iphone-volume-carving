Volume Carving: updated Android frontend for Aaron Wetzler.
Original project: Sergey Kucher and Alex Povichuk, supervised by Aaron Wetzler.
Original source bytes and historical notices remain in the repository.
The maintained native adapter calls the recovered reconstruction operators.
Carving calls the unchanged Sculptor<128> ACCURATE implementation; observed filtering is separate output processing. No triangle extraction is provided.

Dinosaur sequence: Wolfgang Niem, University of Hannover, via Oxford VGG
https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/data/mview/
and Ben Tordoff's example bundled with the recovered Freehand archive.
Calibration: unchanged rounded demoP table, not original Oxford calibration.
Existing source/sample/data terms remain; no new blanket license is assigned.
No camera, pose tracker, general 3D accuracy or captured-device validation claim.
Repository: https://github.com/twerdster/iphone-volume-carving
